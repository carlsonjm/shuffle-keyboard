Try it

      
    
        
  • FlickDrag down on q for 1, on ; for a colon, on [ for a brace.
    • 
        
  • ArrowsTap to move the cursor. Flick ← or → down for Home or End.
    • 
        
  • Hold SpaceKeep your finger down, then move. Up and down change lines.
    • 
        
  • Slide SpaceA sideways drag starts the cursor straight away.
    • 
        
  • Drag delete leftCharacters invert one at a time. Slide back to keep some. Lift to delete.
    • 
        
  • Flick delete leftA quick swipe deletes the last word.
    • 
        
  • Hold eAccents appear. Slide across and lift on one.
    • 
        
  • ☺The emoji panel replaces the letters. ABC brings them back.
    • 
        
  • HideThe keys slide away. Tap the text to bring them back.
    • 
      

    


    

      
From the ThinkPad

      
    
        
  • StaggerThe same steps as today and as ANSI: home row a quarter unit right of the top row, bottom row a further half.
    • 
        
  • Full alpha blockBrackets, backslash, semicolon, quote, minus and equals get their own keys, where a ThinkPad has them.
    • 
        
  • Inverted THalf-height ↑ and ↓ keep the arrows inside the rectangle instead of adding a row.
    • 
        
  • Meta beside CtrlThe Tette Dot sits where a ThinkPad puts its system key, between Ctrl and Alt.
    • 
        
  • TrackPointNot drawn. A pointer nub where g, h and b meet could be the way into pointer mode.
    • 
      

    


    

      
Settled

      
    
        
  1. Pointer. The trackpad mark stays at the space bar's right end. A touch that starts on it latches the pointer; anywhere else on Space types or moves the cursor.
    2. 
        
  2. Del, Page Up, Page Down. On the 123 layer the arrows read Home, PgUp, PgDn and End, and delete reads Del.
    3. 
        
  3. Voice. Takes the emoji key's place once dictation exists.
    4. 
        
  4. Predictions. The top strip is kept clear for them; they come after 1.0.
    5. 
        
  5. The contract. docs/KEYBOARD-CONTRACT.md and docs/INPUT.md state this layout and every input.
